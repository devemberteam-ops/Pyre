# Support

Pyre is free, open-source software maintained by a small team. We read what we can, but there is no guaranteed response time or formal support desk.

## Where to take things

- **Questions and usage help:** use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/devemberteam-ops/Pyre/discussions). Search first; someone may already have hit the same issue.
- **Bugs:** [open an issue](https://github.com/devemberteam-ops/Pyre/issues/new/choose) with steps to reproduce, what you expected, and what happened instead.
- **Docs:** start at [pyrechat.app](https://pyrechat.app).

Please be decent to other users and contributors. Good reports and patient questions make Pyre better for everyone.
